### Duplicate or new?

Every query is scored twice: once with its advisory in the index (a duplicate) and once with it removed (a new bug). Five-fold cross-validation, grouped by query. Cells are the share of duplicates caught while holding precision at the given level.

| decision rule | ROC-AUC | at 95% precision | at 90% | at 80% | best F1 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| top similarity alone | 0.878 | 0.172 | 0.26 | 0.81 | 0.821 (P 0.735, R 0.93) |
| logistic regression: top score, margin, crate named | 0.912 | 0.07 | 0.716 | 0.885 | 0.853 (P 0.844, R 0.862) |

When the bug is new, the best remaining match is another advisory for the same crate 30% of the time. Those look like duplicates on every feature here, which is what limits precision at the top.
